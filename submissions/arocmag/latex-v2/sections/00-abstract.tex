\begin{abstract}
K-Waay的\batchreceive 已要求同次调用输入对应不同参与方。本文构造三种固定两槽Tamarin准入模型。无互异模型允许一个匹配来源对应同批两个接受。消息互异模型的消息互异性和来源单射性均验证通过，但二者在新鲜消息和精确来源匹配下逻辑相关。决定性机器见证表明，同一参与方的不同发送实例和消息各有合法来源，仍能进入同批并产生两个接受，故消息约束不能推出参与方互异性。参与方互异模型保持目标关系且有效批次可达。

\noindent\textbf{关键词：}K-Waay；批次准入；批内参与方互异条件；Tamarin；形式化验证
\end{abstract}

\begin{center}
  \large\bfseries \englishtitle\\[0.5em]
  \normalsize Abstract
\end{center}
\noindent
K-Waay requires the inputs of one \batchreceive invocation to correspond to
different parties.  We construct three fixed-two-slot Tamarin models with a
common lifecycle and, respectively, no distinction constraint, message
distinction, and party distinction.  In the unconstrained model, one matching
sender origin can correspond to two acceptance events in the same batch.  In
the message-constrained model, same-batch message distinction and batch-scoped
origin injectivity are both verified; rule-level analysis shows, however,
that they are logically dependent under the fresh-message and exact-origin
matching rules.  The decisive machine witness is a complete execution in
which two different sender occurrences and messages from the same party, each
with its own legitimate matching origin, enter one batch and yield two
\receiveraccept events.  Thus the message-level restriction does not imply
same-batch party distinction.  The party-constrained model preserves the
target relation while retaining reachable valid distinct-party batches.  The
conclusion is limited to the fixed-two-slot batch-admission abstraction.

\noindent\textbf{Key words:} K-Waay; batch admission; distinct-party
condition; Tamarin; formal verification
