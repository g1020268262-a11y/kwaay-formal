      摘 要：K-Waay 的 BatchReceive 已要求同次调用输入对应不同参与方。本文构造三种固定两槽 Tamarin 准入模型。
      无互异模型允许一个匹配来源对应同批两个接受。消息互异模型的消息互异性和来源单射性均验证通过，但二者在新
      鲜消息和精确来源匹配下逻辑相关。机器见证表明，同一参与方的不同发送实例和消息各有模型内匹配发送来源，仍
      能进入同批并产生两个接受，故消息约束不能推出参与方互异性。参与方互异模型保持目标关系且有效批次可达。

               Formal Analysis of the Distinct-Party Condition in K-Waay Batch Admission

                                                       Author information pending
                                             (Affiliation, city, postal code, and country pending)

      Abstract: K-Waay requires the inputs of one BatchReceive invocation to correspond to different parties. We construct three
      fixed-two-slot Tamarin models with a common lifecycle and, respectively, no distinction constraint, message distinction, and party
      distinction. In the unconstrained model, one matching sender origin can correspond to two acceptance events in the same batch. In the
      message-constrained model, same-batch message distinction and batch-scoped origin injectivity are both verified; rule-level analysis
      shows, however, that they are logically dependent under the fresh-message and exact-origin matching rules. The decisive machine
      witness is a complete execution in which two different sender occurrences and messages from the same party, each with its own
      matching sender origin, enter one batch and yield two ReceiverAccept events. Thus the message-level restriction does not imply
      same-batch party distinction. The party-constrained model preserves the target relation while retaining reachable valid distinct-party
      batches. The conclusion is limited to the fixed-two-slot batch-admission abstraction.
      Key words: K-Waay; batch admission; distinct-party condition; Tamarin; formal verification
