# Paper writing plan

The writing order intentionally differs from the reader-facing section order.
The protocol object, property, abstraction, and results should stabilize before
the Introduction and Abstract summarize them.

1. Section 2 — K-Waay BatchReceive and the Identity Problem
2. Section 3 — Security Objective and Threat Model
3. Section 4 — Formal Modeling Methodology
4. Section 5 — Formal Analysis and Counterexample Traces
5. Section 6 — Discussion and Design Implications, including principal limitations
6. Section 7 — Related Work
7. Section 8 — Conclusion
8. Section 1 — Introduction
9. Abstract

Detailed reproducibility content is written with the appendix rather than as a
numbered narrative section. The next writing phase therefore starts with
Section 2, not the Introduction.
Each phase should convert only the selected source material, reconcile every
claim with its evidence boundary, and leave unrelated research artifacts alone.
